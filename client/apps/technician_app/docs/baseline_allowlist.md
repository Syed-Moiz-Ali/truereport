## D. Known traps for the rebuild

1. **i18n source scan** covers `apps/technician_app/lib`, `packages/auth/lib`,
   `packages/design_system/lib`, `packages/l10n/lib`, `core`, `domain`,
   `api_client`, `offline_sync`. New sign-in widgets must take every
   user-visible string from `AppLocalizations`; no raw `Text('Sign in')`,
   `hint: 'Email'`, `label:`, `title:`, `tooltip:` literals.
2. **Figma sample strings are forbidden in `lib/`** — including the pattern
   list in `test/i18n_source_scan_test.dart` (`SignInCopy`, `Bay 03/04`,
   `David Vance`, `Forgot password?` etc.).
3. **Shared ARB is red before this wave.** Do not try to "fix" the earlier
   placeholder drift inside this wave; that belongs to the workshop wave that
   introduced it. Keep the sign-in change key-neutral.
4. **Login goldens pass today.** A rewrite must regenerate exactly those two and
   show the result at Gate 6; a silently deleted golden test is a hard stop.
